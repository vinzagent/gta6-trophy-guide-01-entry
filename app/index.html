    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). No mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of each chapter. The story is 5 chapters and takes roughly 60–80 hours; the 100% checklist is the long tail after it.

      Stage 1 — Play the story, Chapters 1–5 60–80 hours

      Finish the story and enjoy it. The 100% checklist is not viewable until after the story, but progress made during the story already counts. There are no mission gold medals, so no hidden objectives to chase. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude while you can, scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app, study animals through binoculars, and take every optional mission as it appears. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter: 15 manual slots, no mission replay.

      Stage 2 — 100% completion checklist & 70 of 80 challenges ~100 hours

      After the story the checklist opens under Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. It is lenient, closer to 60% of true completion: most side quests are not required, and collectibles are only partly required. You need 200 of 277 base-model vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, any 70 of 80 challenges and any 100 optional missions. The only categories that need everything are robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests.

      Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and miscellaneous cleanup ~10 hours

      Most trophies should be done by now. Strangers & Freaks are not needed for 100%, so finish the 8 strands that count, then mop up the one-off trophies: the UFO easter egg, the Banshee showroom smash, the VCIA plane theft, the tank, the cocktail on Ocean Beach and the two trophies whose methods are still unconfirmed.

      Stage 4 — Platinum

      Vice Got a Price! pops automatically once the other 54 are earned. No multiplayer, no DLC.

      Missable watch

      Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable by the guides. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story.

      Possibly missable Positive Mental Attitude

      Meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 spots). The catch is a meditation at Jason's house in Chapter 1 that disappears after that chapter. After the Chapter 1 mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning; she says she is going to meditate and walks out to the balcony. Follow her and choose Accept. It adds 1 to the meditation counter but is not marked as a meditation spot like the rest, so it is still unconfirmed whether the trophy actually needs it. Do it anyway while you can: it costs nothing and the safe choice is the only choice when there is no mission replay.

      Source: PowerPyx, GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap (19 Nov 2026); also flagged by GTABase.

      Save insurance (no mission replay)

      • Keep a manual save at the start of each chapter and one before the final mission.
      • There are 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave slot; rotate the spare slots for free-roam cleanup.
      • Optional missions can disappear after a while and some quest givers go quiet for a chapter, but every trophy-related character comes back, so nothing else is missable.
      • Cheats disable trophies and saving, and cheats only unlock after the story anyway: keep them off during a trophy run.

      Source: PowerPyx roadmap (manual saves, no mission replay, cheats).
